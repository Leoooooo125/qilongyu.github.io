I am currently a PhD student in Business Administration (Finance Track) at Stevens Institute of Technology. Previously, I earned a Master’s degree in Quantitative Finance from the University of California, San Diego, a Bachelor’s degree in Finance from Kean University, and a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from Wenzhou-Kean University. My research interests are centered on firm behavior, labor economics, and fintech, with a particular emphasis on causal inference and data-driven empirical analysis.

I have independently led and co-authored several research projects covering topics such as internal pay disparity and analyst forecast accuracy, the impact of racial conflicts on AI adoption in workplaces, and gender differences in analyst performance under political intervention. My work has been presented at esteemed international conferences, including the British Accounting Review Annual Conference and the Vietnam Symposium in Banking and Finance. I am currently preparing multiple manuscripts for submission to peer-reviewed academic journals.

In addition to academic research, I have gained practical industry experience through internships at PwC Consulting, China Securities, and Powerlong Real Estate, where I specialized in risk modeling, financial analysis, and investment evaluation. I am proficient in Python, Stata, R, and Bloomberg, and I am passionate about applying rigorous quantitative methods to real-world financial challenges.

Outside academia, I enjoy music creation, basketball, swimming, and cooking.

I aim to bridge theoretical rigor with applied relevance and actively seek opportunities for collaboration in finance, economics, and societal impact research.
