I am currently a PhD student in Business Administration (Finance Track) at Stevens Institute of Technology. Prior to that, I received a Master’s degree in Quantitative Finance from UC San Diego, a Bachelor’s degree in Finance from Kean University, and a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from Wenzhou-Kean University. My research interests lie at the intersection of firm behavior, labor economics, and fintech, with a particular focus on causal inference and data-driven analysis.

I have independently led and co-authored several empirical research projects examining topics such as internal pay disparity and analyst forecast accuracy, racial conflicts and AI adoption in the workplace, and gender differences in financial analyst performance under political intervention. My research has been presented at international conferences including the British Accounting Review Annual Conference and the Vietnam Symposium in Banking and Finance, and I am currently preparing multiple manuscripts for peer-reviewed journal submission.

Beyond research, I bring practical experience from internships at PwC Consulting, China Securities, and Powerlong Real Estate, where I contributed to risk modeling, financial analysis, and investment evaluation. I am proficient in Python, Stata, R, and Bloomberg, and have a passion for integrating rigorous data analysis with real-world financial insights.

In my spare time, my hobbies are music creation, basketball, swimming and cooking.

I strive to bridge theoretical rigor with applied relevance, and welcome opportunities for collaboration across finance, economics, and society.
